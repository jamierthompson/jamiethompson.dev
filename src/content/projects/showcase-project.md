This project exists to show the `cover` field on a project: it is the
picture on the card on the home page, and the picture under the title here.
The `url` becomes the host link under the title and on the card.

The photo is a placeholder from [picsum.photos](https://picsum.photos/), so
delete this entry and `src/assets/showcase-*.jpg` once a real project is
in.

## What a project page has

Projects are plain `.md` here, where [[this-site]] is MDX. Both get the same
page: the description and date under the title, the link out, the cover, and
the body. A long body gets a table of contents just like
[[everything-a-post-can-show|a post]] does.

## Linking

A project can link to writing, and the post shows the project under
"Referenced by".
